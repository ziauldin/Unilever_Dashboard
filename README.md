# 📦 Unilever Supply Chain Dashboard

A Power BI dashboard that tracks KPIs for Unilever's supply chain and sales: revenue by product type, stock levels, lead times, shipping costs and carriers, manufacturing defect rates, and revenue by customer demographic. It was built to answer the business questions in the problem statement included in this repo.

## 📊 What it shows
- Revenue and units sold by product type and SKU
- Inventory: stock levels, availability, and order quantities
- Logistics: lead times, shipping times, carriers, routes, transport modes, and costs
- Quality: inspection results and defect rates by supplier
- Revenue split by customer demographic

## 🗂️ Data
- `Unilever_Dataset.xlsx`: 100 SKUs with 23 columns covering pricing, sales, inventory, shipping, suppliers, manufacturing, and quality
- `Unilever Problem Statement.docx`: the business problem and the objectives the dashboard addresses

## ▶️ How to open
Install [Power BI Desktop](https://www.microsoft.com/power-bi/desktop) (free, Windows), then open `Unilever Dashboard.pbix`. If Power BI asks for the data source, point it to `Unilever_Dataset.xlsx` in this folder.
